About Us

Empowering Lives. Restoring Hope. Building Tomorrow.

Manatha Foundation is a registered non-profit organisation dedicated to improving the overall well-being of our community by providing essential services, education support, digital access and skills development.

NPO Reg No: 306-164

We are committed to fostering educational excellence, bridging the digital divide, and empowering individuals and families to achieve self-sufficiency and economic stability. Every child deserves equal opportunities for success.

Our Mission

To improve the overall well-being of our community by providing essential services and support systems, fostering educational excellence, bridging the digital divide, and empowering individuals with the skills and resources to thrive in today's world.

Our Vision

A community where every child has equal opportunities for educational success, every individual has access to the tools and skills they need, and every family is empowered to achieve self-sufficiency and economic stability.
