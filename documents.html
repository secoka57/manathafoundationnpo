POPIA & PAIA SECTION 51 MANUAL

In compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA):

  • Personal information collected from donors, volunteers, and beneficiaries is processed lawfully, securely, and strictly for NPO operations.
