Governance

Our Commitment to Accountability

Manatha Foundation is governed in line with its constitution. Our Board of three Office Bearers oversees the organisation's affairs with transparency, integrity and accountability.

Governance Structure

The Office Bearers oversee the organisation and form the Board of governance. The Board is made up of three members as provided for in our constitution.

Board & Office Bearers

Office bearers serve for periods agreed at the Annual General Meeting (not exceeding three years) and may stand for re-election thereafter.

Constitution

Our constitution governs the name, objectives, governance, meetings, finances, amendments and dissolution of the organisation. Approved on 20 March 2024.

Meetings

Annual General Meetings are held once a year toward the end of the financial year. Ordinary and Board meetings are held at least once a quarter.

Financial Governance

The financial year ends on 31 March each year. The Board maintains proper records and accounts, with a report compiled by an independent registered Accounting Officer.

Accountability & Reports

We are committed to transparency. Annual reports, financial reports and governance documents are published for stakeholders to consult.

Official NPO Registration Certificate

Manatha Foundation is officially registered under the Non-Profit Organisations Act, 1997 (NPO Reg No: 306-164).
